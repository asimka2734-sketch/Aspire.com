## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, world-class collegiate presence for a premier institution of higher learning. It balances classical academic tradition with modern institutional agility, instilling confidence, prestige, and scholastic aspiration in prospective students, faculty, and alumni.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Collegiate Prestige** with contemporary functional clarity:
- **Heritage & Gravitas:** High-contrast editorial display styling anchored by traditional academic hallmarks.
- **Structural Integrity:** Deliberate, orderly compositions that project administrative reliability, scholarly rigor, and intellectual permanence.
- **Contemporary Accessibility:** Uncluttered surfaces, airy macro-whitespace, and clean metadata indicators ensuring swift navigation across academic catalogs, admissions workflows, and scholarly records.

## Colors

The color architecture communicates scholarly distinction, dignity, and intellectual excellence:

- **Primary Deep Crimson (`#B5122A`):** The institutional anchor. Used for principal brand moments, prominent interactive focal points, selected states, and pivotal calls to action (such as admissions and enrollment gateways).
- **Secondary Institutional Navy (`#142B45`):** Represents institutional permanence and authority. Used for foundational navigation framing, footers, section backdrops, high-level headers, and structured administrative panels.
- **Accent Warm Gold (`#D6A84F`):** Conveys merit and collegiate achievement. Reserved for honors badges, accreditation markers, deliberate micro-interactions, decorative rules, and active states requiring elevated prestige.
- **Surface Neutral & Canvas (`#F7F8FA`):** Provides a crisp, bright, glare-free foundation. Distinct from pure white cards, allowing elevation layers to register clearly.
- **Body & Text Neutral (`#1E293B`):** Deep slate providing optimal legibility across prolonged reading sessions without the harsh contrast of pure black.
- **Border & Rule Neutral (`#E2E8F0`):** Subtle dividers that maintain clean structural discipline without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes deliberate friction between regal tradition and high-utility functionality:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for institutional statements, program titles, hero headlines, and major section intros. Conveys dignity, history, and academic distinction.
- **Interface & Operational Prose (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean geometry, open counters, and high legibility across dense program catalogs, degree requirement tables, and input forms.
- **Scholastic Labels:** Category tags and departmental monikers pair semi-bold weights with slight positive letter spacing in all-caps or title case to provide clear scanning anchors.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a structured 12-column grid system designed for balanced information architecture:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and generous outer margins (`3rem` standard, expanding up to `6rem` on wide displays) to preserve readability and maintain an intentional editorial cadence. Max content container is capped at 1280px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Multi-column academic cards collapse gracefully into 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Content reflows into single-column flows; horizontal metadata arrays shift into structured vertical stacks.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Macro-sections utilize substantial breathing room (`space-xl` or larger) between major academic departments and news releases to preserve institutional stature.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses subtle surface shifts and diffused ambient shadows rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing an atmosphere of quiet prestige:

- **Base Ground (`#F7F8FA`):** The foundational neutral surface canvas.
- **Surface Tier 1 (`#FFFFFF`):** Base container cards, course modules, and side panels sit directly on canvas, outlined with a razor-thin border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Surface Tier 2 (Ambient Raised):** Interactive cards, program highlights, and active forms gain subtle ambient elevation: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.04)`. The shadow is tinted with Institutional Navy to prevent muddy gray casts.
- **Surface Tier 3 (Floating Overlays):** Dropdowns, modal dialogues, and mega-menus use deeper diffused elevation: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.06)`, maintaining clear separation from the background content.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced **Rounded (`roundedness: 2`)** geometry that softens the formality of classic typography without veering into overly playful territory:

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Primary form inputs, secondary badges, academic program cards, and utility buttons.
- **Containers & Modules (1rem / 16px):** Major content containers, institutional spotlight panels, and admission calculators.
- **Hero & Modal Surfaces (1.5rem / 24px):** High-level featured presentation cards and large modal canvases.
- **Pill Exception:** Status tags, accreditation markers, and high-impact filter pills utilize full pill radiuses to contrast against structural card grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Crimson (`#B5122A`) fill with crisp white text, 8px radius, medium weight typography. Subtly deepens on hover with an ambient crimson glow.
- **Secondary:** Dark Institutional Navy (`#142B45`) fill with white text, providing high authority for administrative or academic actions.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Crisp white background with a 1px border (`#142B45` or `#B5122A`) and corresponding text color.
- **Accent Action:** Warm Gold (`#D6A84F`) fill with Deep Institutional Navy text, reserved strictly for scholarship announcements, merit distinctions, or urgent admissions deadlines.

### Badges & Academic Chips
- Compact, pill-shaped tags featuring 11px uppercase typography with tracking.
- **Faculty/Department Badges:** Pale navy tint with solid `#142B45` text.
- **Honor/Merit Chips:** Warm Gold background tint (`rgba(214, 168, 79, 0.15)`) with `#8F6B20` text and an optional hairline border.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF` encased in a 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Border shifts to Institutional Navy (`#142B45`) on focus, accompanied by a 3px outer glow in pale crimson (`rgba(181, 18, 42, 0.12)`).
- Error states transition the border to solid crimson with a dedicated error caption below.

### Cards & Program Modules
- Pure white background mounted on the `#F7F8FA` canvas, bound by a 1px `#E2E8F0` hairline border and ambient navy shadow.
- Program cards include a top accent highlight (a 3px Crimson or Gold rule) to denote academic tier and department category.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base state: Crisp white fill with a 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border.
- Selected state: Primary Crimson (`#B5122A`) fill with a crisp white checkmark or center pip.

### Tables & Academic Catalogs
- Alternating subtle rows (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`), topped by a solid Institutional Navy (`#142B45`) header row with white uppercase typography for clear data density and legibility.
